# Nova Vanguard Anime — art brief (shared by every character image)

Game: original mobile vertical shooter where pilots are anime girls wearing sci-fi flight rigs ("mecha girl"). Portrait screens, dark navy sci-fi UI (#070a1c, cyan #38e1ff accents).

Style for EVERY character image (identical across the set):
- High-quality modern anime gacha-game illustration: clean thin line art, cel shading with soft gradient highlights, glossy hair highlights, detailed hard-surface mecha parts with panel lines, emissive glow on energy parts.
- Light from top-left, cool cyan rim light from behind.
- Proportions: slim teen-adult heroine, about 1:7 head-to-body for standing art. Large expressive eyes but not chibi (except fly_ sprites).
- Outfit: sci-fi flight suit / jacket + skirt or shorts, tights or thigh-high boots, armored mecha boots with thrusters. The RIG (wings, weapons, thrusters) attaches to the back, hips and arms and uses the character's two colours.
- Content: tasteful, Teen rating. No nudity, no underwear visible, no cleavage focus, no suggestive poses.
- ORIGINAL designs only: do not imitate any existing game, anime or franchise character. No cat/animal ears, no WWII propeller rigs.
- Fully TRANSPARENT background for stand_, face_, fly_ images. No text, logo, signature or watermark.
